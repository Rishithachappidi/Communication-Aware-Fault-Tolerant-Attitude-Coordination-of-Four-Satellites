The single research contribution selected for implementation is the
**reference-relative conditional certificate and uncertainty-aware acquisition
gate**. The estimator change supports experiments on this contribution; it is
not presented as a second invention. The precise claim is conditional sampled
probe-outage control under stated finite-model and measurement/gain assumptions,
with an exact angular worst case for centered ball uncertainty. This is testable
without pretending it establishes a new general safe-diagnosis framework.

A serious submission still requires full-text comparison with the 2026 scanning
paper, a more systematic patent/database search, an independent subject expert,
and matched published constrained-input baselines. If these cannot be completed
within the present environment, the overall research task must be described as
partially complete. There is no basis for an acceptance probability or a claim
that the resulting manuscript meets Transactions novelty standards.
