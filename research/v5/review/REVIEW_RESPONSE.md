## Numerical findings that change the interpretation

With 50 new nominal affine-drift seeds, UAD and fixed margin both have mean outage **5.0324 s**, versus **4.2420 s** for DAP. UAD/fixed margin accept 46/50, DAP 50/50. The simpler selector is not merely similar on averages: its actual trajectories equal UAD in every one of 2,800 paired cases, including the actuator grid. Thus the benchmark does not establish that the extra tube complexity improves operational performance.

At the same 2.88-s excitation-time limit, ES affine-drift outage is **9.8404 s**; native eight-second ES gives **4.2676 s**. Mean angular excitation is approximately **97.92** versus **1713.78 urad²s**. Native mode has more time and excitation. For fast gain, time-matched ES gives **6.9376 s**, native ES **2.4388 s**, while UAD remains **11.8684 s**. Different budgets and tracker/diagnosis objectives are essential to interpretation.

At fivefold noise near sensitivity, DAP has 3/50 false acquisition enablements and **0.08 s** mean outage. UAD and fixed margin abstain and retain zero outage; this is not a diagnosis. In early-fault pointing cases, UAD and fixed margin each retain **50/50 bound failures**, all outside the constant-offset premise. No such adverse result was removed.

Across all original primary+sensitivity UAD records, 2,121 probes have a calculated bound, 1,564 meet checked premises, and zero violations occur in that subset. All 100 original sensitivity violations are among the remaining 557. This does not support a homogeneous binomial flight-risk estimate.
